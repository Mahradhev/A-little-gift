FROM MY HEART TO YOURS

To my lovely man,

I've been trying to find the right words, but every sentence feels too small for the weight of what I feel. With our fourth mensiversary on this another 26th, I thought I would be writing you something full of happiness, little memories, and all the reasons I am grateful to have you. Instead, I find myself writing with a heavy heart, wishing I could take back the words that hurt you yesterday.

I am genuinely sorry, Venor. I am sorry for the things I said, for the pain I caused, and for letting my words become something that could hurt the person I love so deeply. I can't hide behind my emotions or pretend that being upset makes everything I said okay. You didn't deserve to be hurt by someone who should have been more careful with your heart.

I feel guilty knowing that I may have turned a day that was supposed to be special for us into something painful. Our fourth month together was meant to be another little chapter we could look back on with warmth. It breaks my heart that we are approaching it like this, with distance between us, difficult feelings left unanswered, and so much uncertainty about where we stand. I am sorry for my part in bringing us here. I wish I had chosen gentler words. I wish I had paused, listened, and thought about how deeply they might affect you before letting them leave my mouth.

Thank you for coming into my life, for choosing me, and for giving me a place in yours. Thank you for the moments we have shared, the conversations, the affection, and the little things that may have seemed ordinary at the time but mean so much to me now. Thank you for being someone I got to love, someone whose presence became part of my days. I'll never treat the fact that you chose me as something insignificant. Being loved by you has meant more to me than I've always known how to express.

I know we are having our alone time right now, and I wanna respect that. I don't want this gift to become another way of pressuring you into answering me before you are ready. I don't know whether we're still holding on to this relationship or whether we have reached a place neither of us knows how to name yet. That uncertainty hurts, but I know I can't decide how you feel for you, and I can't ask you to ignore your own hurt just because I miss you.

Still, I need you to know that I am sorry. I am sorry not only because I am afraid of losing you, but because I hurt you, and that matters regardless of what happens to us. If you need time, I'll try to understand. If forgiveness takes time, I know I have no right to demand it. I only hope that one day, when the hurt feels a little lighter, you might find room in your heart to forgive me. I know an apology cannot undo what happened, but I want my actions, not just these words, to show that I understand why my words mattered.

I still love you, Venor. Even in this quiet, even in the uncertainty, even while I am sitting with the consequences of my own words, that love is still here. I miss the comfort of knowing we are okay. I miss the thought of celebrating another month with you without all this sadness between us. But more than getting back to how things were, I wish for healing—for you, for me, and for whatever truth remains between us.

So, on our fourth mensiversary, I don't wanna pretend that everything is perfect. I wanna be honest. I am grateful that you happened to find your way into my life. I am grateful that, for these months, we got to call each other ours. And I am sorry that I let my words cast a shadow over a day I wanted to make beautiful for you.

If there is one thing I hope you carry from this letter, it is that you were never insignificant to me. You were never just another passing chapter. You're someone I chose with love, someone I am thankful to have met, and someone I wish I had treated more gently when it mattered most.

I can't promise that these words will fix anything. I can only offer you my sincere apology, my gratitude, and a heart that still wishes you peace, even while it aches. Whatever you need right now, I hope you know that I am sorry for the hurt I caused.

Happy fourth mensiversary, I guess, for us. I wish this letter could have reached you in happier circumstances. I wish I could give you these gifts without a heavy apology attached to them. But this is where we are today, and I wanted to give you something honest rather than pretend that my heart has nothing to regret.

Thank you for choosing me, Venor. Thank you for being part of my life. And, if your heart can find its way there in its own time, I hope you can forgive me.

With all of my heart, and the love that is still here,
Mahradhev.
